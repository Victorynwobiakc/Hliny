How it works

A small sensor on the wall. Help when it's needed.

Hliny keeps watch when no one else is around, so a fall isn't left until morning to be found. No camera, no microphone, nothing to wear.

Four steps

From the wall to the morning report.

  1. 01

    Put it on the wall

    A sensor smaller than a clock sits on the bedroom wall and plugs into a normal socket. There's nothing to wire and nothing to wear.

  2. 02

    It learns the room

    For the first few nights, Hliny gets to know the room on its own. Nobody has to map it, tune it or adjust it.

  3. 03

    It keeps watch

    Whenever no one else is there, it notes when the person you care for is in bed, when they get up, when they go back, and whether they're on the floor.

  4. 04

    It alerts, then it reports

    If they're on the floor and don't get up, Hliny alerts you, their carer or another named contact, so help doesn't wait for morning. Each morning there's also a short timeline of their night.

The morning report

The night at a glance, before they knock.

One short timeline per bedroom. No graphs to interpret and no video to watch.

In bed
When the person you care for settled for the night.
Got up
Each time they left the bed, and for how long.
Back in bed
When they returned.
On the floor
When it started, how long it lasted, and whether a contact was alerted.
Up for the day
When the morning began.

Dignity and privacy

It senses movement, not faces.

Hliny uses radar: low-power radio waves that sense movement and position in the room. It can't take pictures, and it can't hear.

  • No camera

    Nothing records images, so there are none to share or leak.

  • No microphone

    Conversations, the TV and phone calls stay private.

  • Nothing to wear

    No pendant to remember, charge or press.

  • You choose who sees it

    The morning report and any alerts go only to the people you name.

Clear about what it is

What Hliny is not.

Not a medical device

Hliny reports what happened overnight. It doesn't diagnose, treat or predict any condition.

Not an emergency service

Keep any pendant or alarm already in place. If someone is in danger, call 999.

Not a replacement for care

It doesn't replace a single visit. It tells carers what they missed.

Not a promise to prevent falls

It can't stop a fall. If the person you care for is on the floor and stays there, it can alert a named contact instead of waiting for the morning visit.
