For care-at-home providers

Know what happened overnight before your carer walks in.

Hliny is a small plug-in sensor that sits by the bed of each client you care for at home. Every morning it gives your carer a short report: in bed, up, back in bed, or on the floor and for how long. No cameras, no microphones, no installer.

The night gap

Your carers see the day. Nobody sees the night.

Between the last visit at night and the first one in the morning, your clients are on their own. Your carer arrives with questions nobody can answer. Did they get up? Did they fall? How long were they on the floor?

  • 1 in 3 Falls are common. About a third of people over 65, and half of those over 80, fall at least once a year. 1
  • Over half Alone, most can't get help. More than half of older people who live alone can't get up or get help on their own after a fall. 2
  • 8,000 Long lies are rising. In Yorkshire alone, around 8,000 falls a year leave someone on the floor for over an hour, based on 2023 ambulance data. 3
  • — Today's options ask too much. Pendants only help if your client is wearing one and able to press it. Cameras are invasive.
  1. Office for Health Improvement and Disparities, Falls: applying All Our Health, 2022.
  2. Kubitza J et al., BMC Geriatrics 22:582, 2022.
  3. University of Sheffield & Yorkshire Ambulance Service, NIHR Long Lies study protocol, 2025.

Why Hliny exists

Hliny doesn't replace a visit. It fills in the hours between them.

Your carers still do the caring. Hliny tells them what went on while nobody was there, so the first visit of the day starts in the right place.

About Hliny

How it works

Set it up on a routine visit. Read it every morning.

  1. 01

    Plug it in by the bed

    Your carer plugs it into a normal socket. No wiring, no installer, nothing for the client to wear or remember.

  2. 02

    It learns the room

    Over the first few nights it settles into the room by itself. Nobody has to configure it.

  3. 03

    Read the night

    Before each morning visit, a short timeline: in bed, up, back in bed, or on the floor and for how long.

  4. 04

    Alert the people you trust

    If a client is on the floor and stays there, Hliny can alert a named contact, such as your on-call carer or a family member.
