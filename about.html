About Hliny

Technology provides visibility. People provide care.

Hliny exists to help quality care reach more people by making better use of the carers we already have.

Our mission

The night is the gap in care at home.

If your loved one gets care at home, carers visit during the day. Between the last visit at night and the first in the morning, they're usually on their own. We can't put a carer in every home overnight, but we can help the morning visit know what happened.

Hliny doesn't replace a single visit. It gives the carers who are already there the visibility they're missing.

The name

Named after Hlín, the Norse goddess of protection.

In Norse myth, Hlín is sent to protect those in danger. Hliny carries that idea into care at home: it watches over your loved ones through the night, so a fall isn't left until morning to be found.

Who we are

Founded in Aberdeen.

Hliny is based in Aberdeen, Scotland. We're building a way to watch over your loved ones at night, starting with care-at-home providers.

Where we are

Tested in real bedrooms before it's sold.

  1. Now

    Bench testing

    Off-the-shelf hardware in ordinary bedrooms, with the fans, curtains and mirrors that confuse cheap sensors. Staged “on the floor” events, done safely on a mat, against everyday look-alikes like a dropped duvet.

  2. Next

    Ten homes

    A pilot with a care-at-home partner, so the carers who use the morning report shape what's in it.

  3. Then

    Wider availability

    Care providers first, then families.
