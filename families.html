For families

For the nights you can't be there.

If someone you love lives alone, the night is often what you worry about most. Hliny is a small sensor that sits by their bed. If your loved one is on the floor and stays there, it can let you or their carer know, instead of the fall waiting until morning to be found.

Tell us about their nights See how it works

Hliny is in development. We're starting with care providers, and we'd like to hear from families too.

What Hliny does

Quiet all night. Clear in the morning.

  • Watches the night quietly

    A small box by their bed watches from bedtime to morning. There's nothing for them to wear, press or remember.

  • Speaks up when it matters

    If your loved one is on the floor and stays there, Hliny can alert you or their carer.

  • Tells you how the night went

    A short morning report for whoever you choose: in bed, up, back in bed, or on the floor and for how long.

Privacy and dignity

Their bedroom stays private.

Hliny senses movement, not faces. With no camera and no microphone, it watches over your loved one without watching them.

  • No camera

    Nothing records images.

  • No microphone

    Their conversations stay their own.

  • Nothing to wear

    There's no pendant to forget on the bedside table.

  • You decide who sees it

    The report and any alerts go only to the people you name.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I get Hliny now?

Not yet. Hliny is in development, and we're starting with care providers. Tell us about their nights and we'll keep you posted.

Does it replace a pendant alarm?

No. Keep any alarm they already have. Hliny is built for the times nobody presses a button.

Will it film or listen to them?

No. It has no camera and no microphone.

Is it a medical device?

No. It reports what happened overnight. It doesn't diagnose, treat or predict any condition.
